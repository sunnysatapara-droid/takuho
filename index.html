01 — Monozukuri

Thirty years
at the edge

Takuho Industries has ground carbide to the tenth of a micron for three decades. What began as a single grinding line in Aichi is now a full tooling house — CNC turning, VMC machining, and coated-insert production under one roof.

Monozukuri — the craft of making things properly — isn't a slogan here. It's the reason a Takuho edge holds its geometry cut after cut, from the first insert in the box to the last.
